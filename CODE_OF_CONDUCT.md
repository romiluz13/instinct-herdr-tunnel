# Code of conduct

This project is small. The rule is the one you would want in your own workshop: treat everyone who shows up with courtesy, and keep the discussion about the work.

**What we expect**

- Be kind and patient, especially with someone new to Herdr, to terminals or to open source.
- Criticize code, designs and decisions, not people. Say what is wrong and what you would do instead.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant before you object.
- Keep other people's private information private. That includes tokens, tunnel addresses, pane contents and anything from their machine.

**What we do not accept**

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or sexual attention nobody asked for.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or pushing a discussion off the topic until people give up.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.

**Where it applies**

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where you speak for or about this project.

**What happens**

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock a thread, and block someone who keeps breaking these rules. If something is happening to you or you see it, say so in a private report: use the **Security** tab of this repository (**Report a vulnerability** is a private channel to the maintainer) and start the report with "conduct". You will be answered, and what you tell us stays between you and the maintainer unless you agree otherwise.

There is no appeals committee. The maintainer decides, and will explain a decision if you ask politely.
